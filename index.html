      How the ranking works

      Each option starts from published US cost, carbon, timeline and capacity-factor ranges (see sources). The model adjusts them for your region: grid price, interconnection-queue wait and grid carbon; gas prices passed through heat rates; solar and wind cost scaled by local capacity factor; geothermal and hydro by resource quality. Projects bigger than a typical single site add friction. Each criterion is scaled 0–100 across the options and weighted by your priorities. Options that can’t meet your deadline are halved, and non-firm options are cut by 40% when you need 24/7 power. It's a screening tool, not a feasibility study.
